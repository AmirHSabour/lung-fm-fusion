## 1. Motivation

v06 isolated where the tile-level gain comes from: relevance-weighted aggregation gives
0.83–0.85 per model, uniform aggregation ~0.80, whatever the space in which tiles are averaged
(v06 §5: A1 vs A2 vs attention MIL). The relevance weights are learned from 142 slide labels,
through one attention map shared by seven classes, and the three encoders hit the same ceiling.
The best fusion remains the zero-parameter product X1 (0.858; age and sex +0.005, 92.5% on
U, V, G). The next lever is better-supervised relevance weights.

## 2. Design

**One structural change: the experts' aggregator becomes CLAM** (Lu et al., 2021): a new family
`clam`, recipes C1 (single branch, CLAM-SB) and C2 (multi-branch, CLAM-MB), each an expert per
foundation model on that model's tiles without age and sex, combined with the age-and-sex expert
by the X1 product (zero fitted weights). Everything else as v06.

Per tile t: `h_t = Dropout(GELU(W x_t))` (d = 32); gated attention scores (Ilse et al., 2018) with
B branches, `a_{b,t} = softmax_t(w_b · (tanh(A h_t) ⊙ σ(G h_t)))`, B = 1 (C1) or 7 (C2).
- Slide scores. C1: `z = V (Σ_t a_t h_t)`. C2: `z_k = v_k · (Σ_t a_{k,t} h_t) + c_k` (class k uses
  its own attention map).
- Instance clustering loss (CLAM, subtyping form). Each class k has an instance classifier
  `Linear(d → 2)`. For a slide of class y and every class k, take the k_s = 8 most attended tiles of
  branch k (branch 1 for C1): for k = y they are labelled positive and the 8 least attended
  negative; for k ≠ y the 8 most attended are labelled negative. Cross-entropy per (slide, class),
  averaged over classes and slides.
- Loss = 0.7 · class-weighted slide NLL + 0.3 · instance loss (CLAM defaults). Training as v04–v06
  (100 epochs, AdamW lr 1e-3, weight decay 0.01, dropout 0.25, 256 random tiles per step, 3 seeds
  averaged; all 1,024 tiles at prediction). Fixed, not tuned.

Candidates: C1, C2 × {`raw`, `ple`} = 4 (58 in total). Family order: … < mexp < agg < clam.

**Housekeeping, justified by v06 §6 (not structural):**
- the opening adds **both** C1:raw and C2:raw (v06: within-family differences, A1 vs A2 = 0.056,
  were the largest effect, and the opening hid them); 9 openings;
- `mil` joins `untunable_families` (on U, V, G all tuned MIL runs timed out or finished below their
  screen: v04_uvg T015–T017, v06 T018–T020); the tuning step picks among the cheap tunable families.

## 3. Pre-registration

- **Hypothesis:** pseudo-labelled tiles and class-specific attention give the relevance weights
  supervision beyond the slide label, so each expert ranks slides better, and the product of
  better experts with age and sex improves.
- **Predictions:** (1) post-hoc, the C2 UNI2-h expert alone ≥ the attention UNI2-h expert (0.853)
  + 0.01; (2) the best `clam` candidate ≥ X1:raw (0.858) + 0.01; (3) C2 ≥ C1 (class-specific maps);
  (4) the largest per-class gains on Lepidic, Acinar, Papillary; (5) post-hoc, the best `clam`
  candidate beats its own version without age and sex in ≥ 90% of resamples.
- **Success criterion (primary):** the winner clearly beats the current best on U, V, G

  (post-hoc) in ≥ 90% of resamples.

- **Budget:** 20 experiments; 30-minute cap each.

## 6. Analysis

**Hypothesis weakly supported, predictions mostly not met.** (1) C2 UNI2-h expert ≥ 0.863: not
measured for C2; the better recipe C1 gives 0.855 (+0.002). (2) best `clam` ≥ 0.868: no (0.861).
(3) C2 ≥ C1: no (0.852 < 0.861; class-specific maps add 7× the attention parameters and the fold
sd rises to 0.041). (4) gains on Lepidic, Acinar, Papillary: Acinar +0.016, Papillary +0.013,
Lepidic −0.028 (mixed). (5) C1 vs its no-metadata version: 89% (just below). Not falsified (C1 >
X1), but the gain (+0.003, 81%) is below the resolution.

**Mechanism.** The instance loss helps where an expert's relevance map was weakest (Virchow2
+0.009) and not where it was strongest (UNI2-h +0.002). Class-specific attention is worse:
seven maps learned from ~20 slides per class each. The supervision added by pseudo-labels is
self-generated from the same attention, so it sharpens rather than corrects it.

**The invariant of v04–v07.** Every fused model is within ±0.005 of *UNI2-h + age and sex under
the same recipe* (v05 0.856 vs 0.858; v06 0.856 vs 0.858; v07 0.860 vs 0.861). The other two
encoders add information only where UNI2-h is weakest, and not enough to be resolved; age and
sex add +0.003–0.005, in 89–92.5% of resamples. Every expert sees the same 112-µm field of view.

**Feedback loop.** 3 of 5 decisions redirected the search; opening both recipes of the new family
worked as intended (C1 was the second opening of `clam`). No time-outs (MIL untunable). The
tuning step now spends 3 cheap slots on families that cannot win.

## 7. Step back (first principles)

1. **What limits performance now?**
   - *Signal: the field of view.* The label is an architectural pattern (lepidic growth along
     alveolar walls, glands, papillae on fibrovascular cores, cribriform sheets) that pathologists
     read at low power, over hundreds of µm. Every tile is 224 px at 0.5 µm/px = 112 µm, a few
     cells to one alveolus; all three encoders saw only that field, which explains why they are
     redundant (same input scale) and why the residual errors are the architecture-defined
     classes (Lepidic vs In situ vs Acinar vs Papillary). Attention pooling treats tiles as an
     unordered set and discards where they are.
   - *Selection noise:* gains ≤ 0.005 are invisible at n = 162.
   - *Age and sex:* bounded (~+0.005).
2. **Assumptions that could be wrong.** (i) That a set of independent 112-µm tiles carries the
   architecture: test by adding each tile's spatial neighbourhood. (ii) That more encoders give
   new information: they share the scale (v06–v07 weights ≤ 0.1 for V and G).
3. **Directions.**
   - *Spatial context from neighbouring tiles, parameter-free* (simplified graph convolution, Wu et
     al., 2019, on the tile grid; the same neighbourhood as Patch-GCN, Chen et al., 2021): each
     tile token becomes [x_t ; mean of its 3×3 neighbourhood ; mean of its 7×7 neighbourhood]
     (112, 336, 784 µm), computed from stored embeddings on the regular 224-px grid. Attacks the
     field-of-view limit; no foundation model is re-run; no extra parameters except the wider
     input projection. Risk: moderate (3× input width).
   - *Patch-GCN / TransMIL with positional encodings*: learned context; 10⁵–10⁶ parameters for 142
     slides.
   - *Re-embedding at lower magnification*: the direct test of scale, but it re-runs the
     foundation models (excluded by the rules of the loop).
   - *Metadata conditioning the attention* (tokens of age and sex as attention queries): attacks the
     bounded metadata signal; low expected value.
4. **Choice.** Parameter-free spatial context: the one direction that adds information the
   experts have never seen (architecture beyond 112 µm), at no model re-run and almost no added
   parameters.

## 8. Proposal for v08

A tile-context cache: for the same 1,024 cached tiles, every model's embedding concatenated with
the means of the model's embeddings over the 3×3 and 7×7 grid neighbourhoods (tissue tiles only).
A new family `ctx` of experts on context tokens: K1 = CLAM-SB experts (C1 on context), K2 =
attention-MIL experts (the X1 expert on context), both combined with the age-and-sex expert by the
X1 product. Everything else as v07. Specified in `v08.md`.
