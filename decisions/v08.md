# v08: spatial context for every tile (neighbourhood means on the tile grid), parameter-free

Status: designed 2026-11-02. Git tag `v08` (after the run). Config: `conf/versions/v08.yaml`.
Outputs: `artifacts/versions/v08/seed_0/agent/`. Models: U, V, G. Previous version: `v07`
(current best on U, V, G: `v04_uvg`, M2:ft 0.851; highest AUROC so far: v07 C1:raw 0.861).

## 1. Motivation

From v04 to v07 every fused model is within ±0.005 of UNI2-h + age and sex under the same recipe,
whatever the aggregator (attention, composition, CLAM) or the fusion (joint, pooled, product)
(v07 §6). The three encoders are redundant, and the residual errors are the architecture-defined
patterns (Lepidic, In situ, Acinar, Papillary). All inputs share one field of view: 224 px at
0.5 µm/px = 112 µm per tile, and attention MIL treats tiles as an unordered set. Growth patterns
are read over hundreds of µm. The stored embeddings lie on a regular 224-px grid, so the
neighbourhood of every tile is known without re-running any model.

## 2. Design

**One structural change: the tile representation of a new expert family gains spatial context.**

Context cache (stage 08c; same 1,024 tiles per slide as the v04 tile cache, same row order): for
model m and tile t at grid cell (i, j),
`x̃_t = [ x_t ; mean_{|i'-i|≤1, |j'-j|≤1} x_{i'j'} ; mean_{|i'-i|≤3, |j'-j|≤3} x_{i'j'} ]`,
means over the tissue tiles present in the window (the tile itself included), from all of the
slide's tiles, not only the cached ones. Fields of view 112, 336 and 784 µm. Widths: U 4,608,
V 7,680, G 13,824. This is one parameter-free propagation step of a simplified graph convolution
(Wu et al., 2019) on the 8-neighbour tile graph of Patch-GCN (Chen et al., 2021), at two radii.

Family `ctx`, experts per model on context tokens, no age and sex, combined with the age-and-sex
expert by the X1 product (zero fitted weights):

| Recipe | Expert | Same as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K1 | gated-attention MIL | the X1 expert, on x̃ |
| K2 | CLAM-SB (instance clustering loss) | the C1 expert, on x̃ |

Training settings unchanged (d = 32, 100 epochs, 256 tiles per step, 3 seeds, …). Candidates: K1,
K2 × {`raw`, `ple`} = 4 (62 in total); family order … < clam < ctx; opening adds K1:raw and K2:raw
(11 openings); `ctx` untunable. All other families unchanged and on the original cache.

## 3. Pre-registration

- **Hypothesis:** architecture over 0.3–0.8 mm is the information the 112-µm experts lack; adding
  it to every tile improves the architecture-defined classes and makes the experts less redundant.
- **Predictions:** (1) post-hoc, the K1 UNI2-h expert ≥ the X1 UNI2-h expert (0.853) + 0.01; (2)
  the best `ctx` candidate ≥ C1:raw (0.861) + 0.01; (3) mean AUROC over Lepidic, Acinar, Papillary
  up ≥ 0.02 vs the same recipe without context; (4) post-hoc, the best `ctx` candidate beats its
  UNI2-h + age and sex row in ≥ 90% of resamples (fusion no longer ≈ UNI2-h + age and sex).
- **Success criterion (primary):** the winner clearly beats the current best on U, V, G
  (`v04_uvg`) by the leaderboard rule, and beats each of its matched single-model rows (post-hoc)
  in ≥ 90% of resamples.
- **Falsified if:** no `ctx` candidate exceeds its context-free counterpart (K1 vs X1:raw, K2 vs
  C1:raw, same encoding).
- **Budget:** 20 experiments; 30-minute cap each.

## 6. Analysis

**Hypothesis falsified.** No `ctx` candidate exceeds its context-free counterpart (K1 < X1, K2 <
C1). (1) K1 UNI2-h expert 0.844 < 0.863. (2) best `ctx` 0.857 < 0.871. (3) Lepidic/Acinar/Papillary
mean: K1 0.774 vs X1 0.785, K2 0.772 vs C1 0.785, no gain. (4) K2 vs its UNI2-h + age and sex row:
98.7%, but because that row *fell* (0.841), not because the fusion rose.

**Mechanism.** Two readings, both consistent with the records:
- *Variance.* The context token triples the input width (UNI2-h 4,608, GenBio-PathFM 13,824) of
  the one learned projection, for the same 142 slides; fold sd rises from 0.030 to 0.041, and
  5 of 6 experts get worse. This is the third time added capacity lost (v06: three-model
  concatenation 0.854 → 0.834; v07: class-specific attention 0.861 → 0.852).
- *A mean is not an arrangement.* The mean of the neighbouring embeddings describes the local
  tissue mix, not how glands, papillae and alveoli are arranged relative to one another; and at
  0.5 µm/px a 112-µm tile already holds one gland or a few alveoli, the units the encoders were
  trained on.

## 7. Step back (first principles)

1. **What limits performance now?** *Variance of the learned experts.* Every change that adds
   parameters or input width per expert loses (three versions); every zero-parameter change
   (the product X1) is neutral or helps; tuning never helped; fold sd 0.03–0.04. Different
   aggregators and encoders have different per-class strengths (X1 Lepidic 0.786, C1 Acinar /
   Papillary 0.820 / 0.778, Virchow2-CLAM-context 0.860 alone) — their errors are not identical.
   The label itself is noisy: published inter-observer agreement on the predominant pattern is
   moderate (κ ≈ 0.4–0.6; Thunnissen et al., 2012; Warth et al., 2012; values quoted from memory,
   to be checked before the report), which bounds any model.
2. **Assumptions that could be wrong.** (i) That one expert per model is the right unit: the
   learned experts are high-variance estimators of the same function; averaging many is the
   textbook remedy. (ii) That the selection should pick one aggregator: their per-class strengths
   differ.
3. **Directions.**
   - *Ensembles of experts* (product of experts, Hinton, 2002; deep ensembles, Lakshminarayanan
     et al., 2017): average the log-probabilities of every expert type × model, with the
     age-and-sex expert as the prior. Zero parameters; reuses cached experts; attacks variance.
   - *Hierarchical auxiliary target* (IASLC grade groups from the label: In situ; Lepidic;
     Acinar/Papillary; Solid/Micropapillary/Cribriform): shares statistical strength across
     classes. Adds a head; moderate.
   - *More training slides*: the cohort has ~180 further slides of the same patients (D5); using
     them as extra bags would directly cut variance, but needs new embeddings (the foundation
     models re-run) and a protocol change: requires the user's approval; not in this loop.
   - *Learned context (Patch-GCN, TransMIL)*: more parameters; v08 says no.
4. **Choice.** Ensembles of experts: the only direction that cuts variance with zero parameters and
   no new data, and it combines the four sources in the most standard probabilistic form.
